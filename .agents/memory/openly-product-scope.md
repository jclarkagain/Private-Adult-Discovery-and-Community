---
name: Openly product scope
description: Product scope and inclusion requirements stated for Openly.
---

Openly is a personal adult discovery and community product intended to welcome people across genders and identities. The requested range includes dating and personals, classifieds, cruising, queer and kink communities, adult books and novelty, and sharing.

**Why:** These are the creator's stated scope and inclusion requirements; the product should not be narrowed to one demographic or only dating.

**How to apply:** Keep discovery categories broad, identity and interests self-described and optional, and privacy and consent central.
